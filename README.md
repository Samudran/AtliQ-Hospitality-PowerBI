# AtliQ Grands – Power BI Dashboard for Hospitality Analytics

## 🧩 Business Problem

AtliQ Grands is a luxury five-star hotel chain operating across India for the past 20 years. In recent times, they’ve been facing a decline in both **market share** and **revenue** in the luxury/business hotel segment due to stronger competition and ineffective internal decision-making.

To tackle this, the managing director has decided to adopt **Business and Data Intelligence** practices. However, lacking an in-house data analytics team, they outsourced the task to an external data analyst — you.

---

## 🎯 Objective

As a data analyst, your tasks were to:
- ✅ Develop business-critical **metrics** as per stakeholder requirements.
- ✅ Build a **Power BI dashboard** based on mock-up guidelines.
- ✅ Go beyond the brief and uncover **additional insights** from the data.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI** – For building interactive dashboards  
- **Microsoft Excel** – For initial data exploration and cleaning  
- **DAX** – For calculated metrics and logic

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains:
- 📌 Occupancy Rate and ADR (Average Daily Rate)  
- 📌 Revenue by Market Segment and Property  
- 📌 Weekly and Property-wise Trends  
- 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

> Screenshots of the dashboard can be found in the `/Images` folder.

## 🖼️ Dashboard Preview

Below are the main sections of the Power BI dashboard created for AtliQ Grands:

### 1️⃣ Overview Dashboard

![Overview Dashboard](Resource/Images/AtliQ%20Hospitality%20Dashboard.png)

The first page of the dashboard gives a high-level summary of the hotel chain's performance. At the top, you'll find KPI cards displaying critical metrics such as **Total Revenue**, **Occupancy Rate**, and **Average Rating**, helping stakeholders quickly grasp overall trends.

Just below, these same metrics are broken down by **city**, offering a comparative view of how each location is performing in terms of revenue generation, occupancy levels, and customer satisfaction.

To the right, an **area chart** tracks weekly trends in **occupancy** and **average ratings** across months, giving insight into seasonal patterns and service consistency.

In the lower section, a combination of **pie charts** and a **bar chart** reveals guest behavior patterns — like how bookings vary by **day type** (weekday/weekend) and **platform** (such as direct or third-party channels).

Users can also interact with the page using filters for **property**, **city**, **booking status**, **platform**, and **month**, making it easier to drill down into specific dimensions of the business.

---

## 📸 Dashboard Preview – Property-Level Analysis

![Property-Level Dashboard](Resource/Images/AtliQ%20Hospitality%20Dashboard(2).png)

This section provides a focused view on the **performance of each property** using key operational and financial metrics. A comprehensive **table** showcases details such as **Property ID**, **City**, **Average Rating**, **Occupancy %**, **Cancellation %**, and **Total Revenue**, helping stakeholders benchmark performance across properties.

Supporting visuals include a **bar chart** that breaks down **booking percentages by room class** and a **column chart** representing **revenue by property name**. 

Standard filters such as **City**, **Property**, **Platform**, **Status**, and **Month** are also available to help refine the analysis.

---

### 🧾 Dashboard Page 3 – Channel & Day Type Analysis

![Channel & Day Type Analysis](Resource/Images/AtliQ%20Hospitality%20Dashboard(3).png)

This page provides a comprehensive breakdown of key performance metrics across different booking channels and day types. It displays KPIs such as **Revenue**, **RevPAR**, **DSRN**, **Occupancy %**, **ADR**, and **Realisation %**, summarized in easy-to-read cards.

Below the KPIs, a table compares these metrics for **weekday vs weekend** bookings, helping to identify behavioral patterns.

You'll also find a **combo chart** that illustrates how **Realisation %** and **ADR** vary across booking platforms, and a **donut chart** that highlights the **revenue contribution by hotel category** (luxury vs business).

> For more detailed visuals, refer to the complete `.pbix` file or explore all screenshots in the `images` folder.

## 🚀 Business Impact

This dashboard empowers AtliQ Grands’ leadership to make data-informed decisions by:

- 📌 Identifying underperforming properties based on revenue, occupancy, and guest ratings
- 📌 Understanding customer booking behavior by platform, day type, and room class
- 📌 Recognizing seasonal and weekly trends in performance to adjust pricing and marketing strategies
- 📌 Monitoring key metrics like ADR, RevPAR, and Realization% to optimize profitability
- 📌 Spotting potential issues early through high cancellation rates or low occupancy figures

By consolidating all critical metrics into a single interactive view, the dashboard enables faster, smarter, and more proactive decision-making.
